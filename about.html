About BCM Events

Upcoming events, talks, socials, sign-ups, and deadlines announced in BCM student GroupMe chats, gathered in one place. It refreshes every couple of hours from the class and student-org chats listed below.

Unofficial and student-run. Details are read from announcements and flyers automatically and double-checked once a day, but mistakes happen: confirm the time and place with the organizer, especially anything marked Double-check.
