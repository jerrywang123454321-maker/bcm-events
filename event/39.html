BCM '30 Houston · Sep 10
Hi all, CTSIG will be holding our first journal club on Sunday October 6 on Teams. This is a great opportunity to learn about CT topics that you will not encounter unless you do a dedicated CT rotation. This will also be a chance learn a little about complex aortic surgery, something that Baylor is particularly known for! We will discuss a paper on cerebrospinal fluid drainage in thoracoabdominal aortic aneurysm repair, followed by a techniques presentation on cerebral protection strategies during aortic arch replacement. All class years welcome! If you would like to help lead discussion, please sign up for the "Discussion Leader" role!
