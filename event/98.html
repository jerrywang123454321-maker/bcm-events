BCM '30 · Sep 29
Hello MS1s! The AOA Carolyn L. Kuckein Student Research Fellowship info session will be on Tuesday, October 6, from 7:30–8:00 PM. The fellowship provides funding for medical student research in clinical investigation, basic science, epidemiology, social science/health services research, and leadership or professionalism. We’ll discuss the fellowship in more detail at the meeting, including eligibility, the application process, and tips for preparing a strong mini proposal. Malay Shah, a previous fellowship recipient, will also be joining us to share his experience and answer questions. Mini proposals will be due Thursday, October 22, 2026. Please see the attached flyer for an overview. Hope to see you there!
