Med Mentor Night (M&M Night)- On behalf of Rice University’s RPMS Mentorship Committee, we would like to invite you to be a mentor for the fall semester of the 2026-27 school year! M&M night is a medical mentorship program where healthcare professionals and medical students can share their knowledge and experiences (medical school applications, interviewing tips, day-to-day lifestyle, aspirations, medical ethics, health policy, and much more) with Rice undergraduates through small group-style conversations. Our first mentorship meeting of the school year will be in person on Thursday, September 24th from 6:30 pm-7:30 pm at Rice University. Food, refreshments, and parking reimbursements will be offered. If you'd like to attend this meeting, please fill out this form (https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSe31UIn8f7rR4GJ2C4-BVwo3KN8a1z-ExKYm7CVHrhtyDLF5A/viewform) with your availability. The logistics will be sent out by the end of the week before the meeting detailing parking and arrival instructions.
